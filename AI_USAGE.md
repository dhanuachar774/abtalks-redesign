# AI Usage Log – ABTalks Redesign

## AI Tool Used
ChatGPT

## Purpose of AI Usage

AI was used during the development of the ABTalks redesign for:

- Brainstorming UI/UX ideas
- Generating and refining React components
- Creating CSS styling
- Debugging navigation and authentication issues
- Improving responsive design
- Troubleshooting deployment issues
- Preparing project documentation

## Development Process

The AI suggestions were reviewed, modified, tested, and integrated into the project based on the requirements of the ABTalks redesign challenge.

## Human Contribution

The project design decisions, implementation choices, testing, integration, and final submission were reviewed and completed by the developer.
